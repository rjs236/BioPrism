Scientific Integrity & Methodology Notice:

To strictly prevent data leakage (Type III error / optimistic bias), all feature normalization (StandardScaler) is encapsulated strictly within cross-validation folds using sklearn.pipeline.Pipeline. No test fold statistics were exposed during feature scaling or model fitting. Computational biomarker discovery prioritizes candidates for subsequent prospective bench validation (RT-qPCR, immunohistochemistry).
